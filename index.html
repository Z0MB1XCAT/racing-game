          How to play

          Driving

          • Your car always accelerates, same as the original. You only steer: ← → or A D.
          • Turning hard is how you slow down, so turn in early.
          • Stuck or spun round? R puts you back on the track, stopped. If you get knocked outside the walls, that happens on its own.
          • On a phone, tilt to steer or switch to tapping the screen sides in Settings.
          • Slipstream: tuck in right behind another car and you get towed along a little faster (watch the meter under your speed). Pull out at the last moment to pass. The car you're pushing gets a small boost too.

          Modes

          • Race: most laps first wins. Everyone else gets 30 seconds to finish after the winner.
          • Elimination: each time the leader completes a lap, the last car is out.
          • Time trial: race a ghost of your best lap. Your fastest laps go on the track's public lap record board.
          • Leaderboards: wins and podiums from online races with at least two real drivers.

          Keys

          • Hold B to look behind you. The mirror at the top shows who's close (switch it off in Settings).
          • C change camera · M mute · Esc pause

          Physics and the Classic track are from jchabin/cars (GPL-3.0). Circuit shapes are simplified from the real layouts.
